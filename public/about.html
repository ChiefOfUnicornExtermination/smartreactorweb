Our Story

Founded in 2020, Smart Device began with a simple question: what if smart home technology was actually simple? We set out to create products that anyone could understand and use, without needing a manual or tech support.

Today, thousands of households trust Smart Device to bring intelligence, security, and peace of mind to their homes. We're committed to continuous innovation and customer satisfaction.
